# meanings
SEO = search engine optimisation (ranking in Google search)
AEO = answer engine optimisation (being quoted in AI answers and AI overviews)
GEO = generative engine optimisation (being cited by AI tools)
ASO = app store optimisation (ranking and converting in the App Store and Play Store)
CSL = custom store listing (alternative app store pages for different keywords)
CRO = conversion rate optimisation
CTI = click to install rate
ORM = online reputation management
TMB = a keyword score the team uses
PLTV = predicted lifetime value of a user
M0 revenue = revenue in the first month
